Italian home cooking

Your kitchen, organised.

GustOH brings order to your kitchen: step-by-step recipes, weekly meals, shopping list and pantry, all in one app.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

What it does

  • Step-by-step recipes

    Traditional Italian recipes explained in plain words, with guided cooking mode: one step at a time, with timers.

  • Weekly meals

    Plan what to eat over the next few days and see it all at a glance.

  • Shopping and pantry

    A shopping list sorted by aisle and a pantry that tells you when something is running low.

  • "Dinner at…"

    Tell the app when you want to eat and it tells you when to start cooking.

  • AI chef

    Ask for a dish or tidy up one of your recipes. Adults only; answers may contain mistakes and we always say so.

  • Courses and community

    Short lessons on techniques and kitchen tools, and a community to share your home cooking.

Made for everyone

  • People who have never cooked
  • People who cook every day
  • People who train and follow a plan
  • People who cook like a chef

Your data is yours

  • No automatic ads: sponsored content is always labelled "Advertising".
  • Export your recipes and data whenever you like.
  • Delete your account and data with one tap.
  • Servers in the European Union.

Some features require a Premium subscription, with a 7-day trial: length and price are shown before you start.
