Your kitchen, organised.

GustOH brings order to home cooking: step-by-step recipes, weekly meals, shopping list and pantry. All in one app, in English and Italian.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

Tell us when you want to eat. We tell you when to start.

Every recipe knows its timings. Pick the time you sit down and GustOH tells you when to turn on the stove, step by step.

Recipes explained the way someone at home would

Traditional Italian recipes in plain words. Servings rescale, timings are clear, and guided cooking shows one step at a time, with that step's ingredients and a timer.

Your week, shopping and pantry talk to each other

Plan the week's meals and the shopping list writes itself, sorted by aisle and without what you already have. The pantry tells you when something is running low.

And also

An AI chef

Ask for a dish with what you have, or tidy up one of your recipes. Adults only: answers may contain mistakes and we always say so.

Short courses

A few minutes per lesson on techniques and kitchen tools. At the end you try a real recipe.

A home cooking community

Share your dishes and cook other people's. Recipes with allergens you excluded never appear.

Made for anyone who cooks, at any level

  • Learn

    Start from zero: easy, guided recipes, one step at a time.

  • Get organised

    You cook every day: week, shopping and pantry in order.

  • Eat well

    You follow a plan or want to eat healthy and high-protein.

  • Cook like a chef

    More ambitious dishes and dinners with guests, well timed.
