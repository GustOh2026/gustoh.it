Delete your account

App: GustOH · Developer: Marketing Genetics S.r.l.

In the app

  1. Open GustOH and tap the gear at the top right of Home (Settings).
  2. Open Account and sync.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

If you created a family, before the account is deleted the family passes to the person who joined first, so the others keep the shared shopping list.

Without the app

Write to supporto@gustoh.it from your account's email address with the subject "Delete account". We delete the account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address.
  • Data stored online: your recipes, favourites, weekly meals, shopping list, pantry, journal, settings, allergies and meal plan.
  • Your community profile and what you published, photos included.
  • Your private chef listing, if any, and the documents you uploaded.

What is kept

  • An unreadable fingerprint of your email (not the email itself) with the number of chef requests already used, so that deleting and recreating the account does not grant extra requests. Retention: 24 months after last use, then it is deleted automatically.
  • For people removed from the community for breaking the rules, the same fingerprint prevents creating a new profile.

Data only on your phone

Without an account your data is only on your phone: delete it from Settings → Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app.

Subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: cancel it in your Apple or Google account settings.
