The service

GustOH helps you organise your home kitchen: recipes, weekly meals, shopping list, pantry, cooking timings and an AI chef. Service operator: Marketing Genetics S.r.l., Via Guido Baccelli 44, 41126 Modena (MO), Italy, VAT no. IT04020560365, email supporto@gustoh.it.

Subscriptions and free trial

  • Some features require a Premium or Family subscription.
  • The free trial lasts 7 days. Duration and price after the trial are shown before you start. We remind you 2 days before it ends.
  • The subscription renews automatically for the same period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before renewal, in your Apple or Google account settings.
  • Payment is handled by Apple or Google under their terms; refunds follow their rules.
  • Price lock: if prices go up, existing subscribers keep their price as long as the subscription stays active without interruption.
  • The Couple plan is shared with 1 other person; the Family plan with up to 4 people, or 6 with the "Extra person" add-on.

Recipes, health and safety

  • Recipes are home-cooking guidance. Always check labels, allergens, cooking temperatures and storage.
  • AI-generated recipes may contain mistakes: they are marked with a warning.
  • The app does not give medical advice or create diets: a meal plan must be prescribed by a professional; the app only organises it.
  • The allergen filter helps but does not replace checking ingredients and labels.

Fair use

Age

Anyone can use the app. The AI chef (recipes on request and reading your meal plan) is for adults only: the first time, we ask you to confirm you are at least 18.

Proper use

Do not use the app unlawfully or try to bypass limits or misuse the AI chef. We may suspend accounts that do.

Content

Texts, catalogue recipes, design and brand belong to the operator. Recipes you create remain yours; you can export them at any time.

Community

Reading the community is open to everyone. To publish recipes, photos and comments you need an account and must be at least 18 (declared), and you must accept the community rules: zero tolerance for offensive, sexual, violent or hateful content, advertising, personal data and dangerous advice. All content goes through an automatic check (including AI) before it becomes visible; reports are reviewed within 24 hours and anyone breaking the rules is excluded. By publishing you allow the operator to show the content in the app until you remove it (you can do so at any time). Imported recipes are rewritten in new words before they become public. Only publish photos you took.

Challenges

Community challenges are free activities just for fun: there are no prizes in cash, goods or vouchers, no draws and no purchase required. Participants only receive a badge on their profile ("Participant"; "Community pick" for the entries with the most likes, calculated automatically when the challenge closes). Entries follow the community rules and may be removed if they are off-topic or break the rules. The operator may change dates or cancel a challenge.

Private chef directory

The directory is only a list of professional private chefs. Before publication the operator checks by hand that the chef has provided a valid VAT number and an HACCP certificate, but does not verify the quality of the service, prices, local permits or insurance. GustOH does not handle bookings or payments and takes no commission: every arrangement (menu, price, date, payment, any allergies) is a direct contract between the user and the chef, who is solely responsible for it. Chefs declare that they are at least 18, that they own the business, that they hold and keep valid HACCP training, that they comply with hygiene and tax rules (invoicing, VAT) and that their details are true; they must update or remove their listing if anything changes. The operator may suspend or remove a listing following reports, invalid documents or rule violations.
