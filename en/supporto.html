Frequently asked questions

Do I need to sign up?

No, you can start right away. Without an account your data stays only on your phone. With an account (email code, Apple or Google) you get it on all your devices and can use family, community and the chef.

How do I export my data?

In the app: Settings → Export my data. You can save your recipes, journal and lists as text or PDF at any time.

How does the subscription work?

Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled by the App Store or Google Play. The trial lasts 7 days: length and price after the trial are shown before you start, and we remind you 2 days before it ends. To stop renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before it expires in your Apple or Google account settings.

How does the allergen filter work?

If you choose allergens to exclude, recipes containing them never appear: it is a fixed rule based on the ingredients, not an estimate. Always check product labels anyway.

Who can use the AI chef?

Adults only: the first time we ask you to confirm you are at least 18. The chef's recipes may contain mistakes and are always marked with a notice.

How do I delete my account?

All the steps are on the Delete your account page.
