Who processes your data

Data controller: Marketing Genetics S.r.l., Via Guido Baccelli 44, 41126 Modena (MO), Italy, VAT no. IT04020560365, email supporto@gustoh.it.

What data we use and why

  • Data you enter in the app (favourites, weekly meals, shopping list, pantry, journal, your recipes, preferences): needed for the app to work. It stays on your phone; if you create an account it is also stored on our server to sync your devices.
  • Account: if you create one, your email address (or your Sign in with Apple identifier, or your Google account email if you sign in with Google) is used to sign in and sync your data. With Google we only receive your name, email and profile picture; we never receive your password.
  • Family: if you join a family, weekly meals, shopping list and pantry are visible to and editable by the family members.
  • Allergies and intolerances (health data): used only with separate consent, to hide unsuitable recipes and products. They stay on your phone and, if you have an account, in your personal account too so you have them on all your devices (the family can't see them); when you use the AI chef (recipes, chat, import, supermarket photos) your excluded allergens are sent with the request so it can avoid them.
  • Meal plan (health data): only with separate consent. It stays on your phone and, if you have an account, it is also stored on our server so you have it on all your devices (it is not shared with the family). If you choose to photograph it, the photo or PDF is sent to our server and to the AI provider only to read it; we do not keep it.
  • Shops where you buy groceries (if you add them) and pantry product photos (if you take any): they stay on your phone and are not sent to anyone.
  • AI chef requests: what you write, the photos you choose to send (receipts, products, labels, book pages, meal plan) and the links of recipes you import are sent to our server and to the AI provider only to answer; we do not keep the photos. To track costs we record how many requests you make and their length, not their content.
  • Barcode: the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (an open product database) to get the product's name, ingredients and nutrition; like any website, Open Food Facts also receives the request's IP address.
  • Anonymous identifier and count of AI chef requests: used to prevent abuse and apply your plan's limits. For the same reason, for non-subscribers, we keep an unreadable fingerprint of the IP address (not the address itself) for at most 2 days.
  • Subscription: handled by Apple or Google; we only receive its status (active, trial, expired) via our provider RevenueCat. We never receive your card details.

Challenges, creator stats and the private chef directory

  • Challenges: the photos and recipes you enter and the likes you give are linked to your community profile; others see the entry and how many likes it has, not who gave them.
  • Creator stats: when you open or save a community recipe we count one view (once a day) or one save, to show the author the totals. We keep who viewed a recipe for at most 2 days (only to avoid counting twice), then only the numbers remain; authors never see who viewed or saved.
  • Private chef directory: if you apply as a chef we process your public listing (your name or business name, city and areas, styles, description, price range and the contacts you choose to show: phone, email, website) and, only to verify you, your VAT number and HACCP certificate (number, date and the document you upload). VAT number and documents are never public: only you and the people who review applications on behalf of the operator can see them. Listing texts go through Anthropic for the automatic check. You can remove your listing at any time; deleting your account deletes the listing and documents. Legal basis: performance of the service you request and legitimate interest in listing only verified professionals.

Legal bases

  • Performance of the contract (running the app and subscription).
  • Explicit consent for health data (allergies, meal plan), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.
  • Legitimate interest for security and abuse prevention.

Who receives data (processors)

  • Supabase (server and database, European Union region).
  • Community: what you publish (chosen name, @name, bio, avatar, recipes, ratings, comments, photos) is visible to everyone; texts and photos go through Anthropic for the automatic check before publication. Emails are never shown.
  • Anthropic (AI chef): receives only the content of requests; it may keep it for a limited time for safety, under its terms.
  • RevenueCat, Apple, Google (subscriptions).
  • Resend (sending the sign-in code emails, European Union region): receives only your email address and the code.
  • Sentry (app error reports, European Union region): receives only the technical description of the error and the phone model, without name, email or IP address.
  • Open Food Facts (only the barcode of products you scan).

Some providers may process data outside the EU with GDPR safeguards (standard contractual clauses).

How long

Data on your phone stays until you delete it. Account data stays while the account is active; if you delete it, it is erased within 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export (Settings → Export my data), delete your data from the phone (Settings → Delete all my data) or delete your account and all server data (Account → Delete account), object to or restrict processing and withdraw consent. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The app (recipes, week, shopping, pantry) can be used by everyone; children under 14 need a parent's consent to create an account. The AI chef is for adults only.

Advertising

GustOH uses no automatic advertising or ad profiling. Any sponsored content is always labelled "Advertising".

Contact

For any privacy request: supporto@gustoh.it.
